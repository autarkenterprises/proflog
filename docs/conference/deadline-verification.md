### LOPSTR+PPDP format notes (official)

- Mark submission as **System Description** where applicable.
- System descriptions: **≤10 pages** (excl. bibliography); must link to working system.
- Long papers ≤15 pages; short papers ≤8 pages.
- Springer LNCS format.

### miniKanren format notes (official)

- `acmart` / `acmsmall`; anonymous review option.
- Short ~2–7 pages; long ~8–25 pages (rough guideline).
- Proceedings on **arXiv**; re-publication elsewhere allowed.

### LPAR format notes (official)

- Regular ≤15 pages EasyChair style; tool/experimental ≤8 pages with artifact link.
- Short presentation track deadline **17 Aug 2026** (informal proceedings).
