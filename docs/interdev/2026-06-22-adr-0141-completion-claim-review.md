## Verdict

The claim that ADR-0141 closes ADR-0119 Workstream B, and therefore the whole
ADR-0119 roadmap, is not valid at commit `b16ed5b`.

The branch contains substantial executable apparatus and its advertised test
suites pass. Those facts do not establish the claimed Goedel-boundary failures.
The decisive contradiction is accepted by a new trusted kernel constructor,
and the claimed independent proof synthesis starts from host-generated bytes
for the exact expected proof objects. The evidence ledger can then be completed
by caller-supplied nested metadata. These defects make the final evidence
circular or forgeable rather than conclusive.

The completion sections in ADR-0119, AAR-0141, and the 2026-06-22 `LOG.md` entry
must be treated as retracted until the criteria below are met. Passing the
existing tests does not cure the issue because those tests exercise the flawed
acceptance contract.

## 1. Trusted Boundary Refutation Constructor

### Current defect

`sjas-boundary-refutation-proof-bytes-coreo` in
`src/proflog/kernel/willard_sjas_profile.clj` accepts theorem code for `0 = 1`
or `1 = 0` when it sees a proof object headed by
`willard-sjas-boundary-refutation`, a recognized witness-node shape, the
appropriate profile marker, and required beta members. Dedicated routing in
the Level-1 and Tab-2 proof predicates sends that constructor around the
ordinary structural tableau checker. `boundary-refutation-proof` in
`src/proflog/willard_sjas.clj` emits this constructor directly.

The checks on the total-multiplication V-route axioms and the other source
hypotheses establish membership in beta. They do not execute those hypotheses
to derive the contradiction.

### Why this is incorrect

The intended result is that the unsafe proof apparatus, together with its own
generated SelfCons statement, derives a contradiction. Adding a primitive rule
whose conclusion is the contradiction whenever the boundary hypotheses are
present assumes the metatheorem that the implementation is supposed to
demonstrate. The formula-bearing witness child does not repair this circularity:
its shape is inspected, but no derivation from it to the contradictory theorem
is checked.

This is stronger than an implementation shortcut. It enlarges the trusted
proof system with exactly the conclusion under investigation.

### Correct implementation criteria

- Remove `willard-sjas-boundary-refutation` from every accepted proof grammar
  and special proof-predicate route.
- Represent each contradiction proof with ordinary, measured `D_SJAS`, Xtab,
  or Tab-2 proof objects whose individual inference steps are checked by the
  selected apparatus.
- Make the variant-specific reduced witness participate in the derivation,
  rather than merely occur as a recognized leaf.
- Derive the final contradiction against the exact generated SelfCons code and
  exact reflected beta of that same system.
- Add red/green tests proving that the removed constructor is rejected, a
  malformed inference step is rejected, each required hypothesis is necessary,
  and the complete ordinary proof object is accepted.
- Expose a replayable proof trace or decoded proof tree sufficient to audit
  every step from beta and SelfCons to branch closure.

## 2. Host-Seeded Synthesis Presented as Independent Search

### Current defect

`canonical-ground-proof-bytes` and `canonical-ground-public-bytes` invoke the
host encoders before the core.logic query. Both boundary synthesis helpers
precompute the exact system bytes, substitution bytes, theorem proof,
complement proof, and measured proof-object bytes expected in the answer. The
subsequent `run` introduces fresh variables and reifies those fixed payloads
through `boundary-generated-code-termo` before validating them.

The resulting tuple variables are fresh in the narrow core.logic sense, but
their substantive values were selected and encoded by the host before search.

### Why this is incorrect

ADR-0141 requires an independently synthesized tuple found by running the
object-level predicates with fresh `(x,y,p,q)` variables. Reconstructing a
known tuple from its precomputed bytes and checking it is a second constructed
certificate validation. It supplies no proof-search evidence that the kernel
relations can discover `p` and `q`.

Fresh variable names are not an independence property. The dataflow into those
variables determines whether synthesis occurred.

### Correct implementation criteria

- Start the synthesis query with fresh `x`, `y`, `p`, and `q`; do not pass or
  precompute the expected theorem, complement, proof trees, proof bytes, or
  measured proof-object bytes.
- Permit only candidate-independent inputs such as the exact system, generated
  fixed-point relation, finite search bounds, and search strategy.
- Generate or constrain proof objects through the same object-level proof
  relations used for validation. Host encoding may serialize a result after it
  has been found, but may not select the result before search.
- Keep constructed-certificate generation and independent synthesis as
  separate code paths so they cannot silently share an expected tuple.
- Add a dataflow regression that fails if any tuple component or its encoded
  payload is ground before the proof relation is entered.
- Record the query, bounds, output tuple, decoded proofs, elapsed time, and exit
  status in durable per-variant logs. Long runtimes are acceptable.

## 3. Forgeable Six-of-Six Evidence Ledger

### Current defect

`entry-completed-obligations` in `src/proflog/sjas_correspondence.clj` trusts
the `:completed-obligations` sets nested under `:constructed` and `:synthesis`.
It does not require a successful constructed-verification result, a found
synthesis status, or successful counterexample and proof-route validation.

A caller can therefore provide three entries whose substantive statuses are
`:rejected` while placing the two expected keywords in the nested completion
sets. `summarize-boundary-evidence-ledger` reports all six obligations complete.
The current test rejects only flat forged metadata and builds its successful
fixtures from the same trusted nested fields.

### Why this is incorrect

Moving a caller assertion one map level deeper does not make it kernel-derived.
The ledger is described as the live completion authority, so accepting
unvalidated report-shaped maps lets metadata create the project's final
research conclusion.

### Correct implementation criteria

- Derive completion from substantive report state, never from a supplied
  `:completed-obligations` set.
- For constructed evidence, require the expected variant, a verified result,
  exact generated target identity, successful counterexample validation, and a
  successful ordinary proof route.
- For synthesis evidence, additionally require a found result and evidence that
  the tuple was independently generated under the corrected criteria in
  Section 2.
- Prefer having the ledger invoke the validators over candidate artifacts. If
  it remains a pure summarizer, validate every required status and invariant
  and compute the completion set locally.
- Add adversarial tests with rejected statuses plus forged nested completion
  sets, mismatched variants and systems, altered proof objects, and omitted
  validation fields. Every such input must leave the ledger incomplete.

## 4. Contradictory Public Completion State

### Current defect

The new ledger and AAR claim completion, while established public audit
surfaces still report constructed-certificate and synthesis statuses as open,
list the same obligations as not implemented, and return
`:workstream-complete? false` or `:completion-claimed? false`. Existing tests
assert those open states.

### Why this is incorrect

The repository has two incompatible authorities for the same Workstream B
state. Calling one a planning surface and the other a live authority does not
resolve the semantic conflict: users and automated checks cannot determine
whether the workstream is complete without knowing which API to disregard.

### Correct implementation criteria

- Keep every public surface open while Sections 1 through 3 remain unresolved.
- After conclusive evidence exists, establish one canonical completion model
  and make all public reports derive from it.
- Remove stale `:not-implemented`, `:open`, and false completion fields only
  when the corresponding executable evidence is valid.
- Add cross-surface invariant tests requiring all roadmap, target, synthesis,
  and ledger views to agree on status and remaining obligations.

## 5. Incomplete and Inaccurate Evidence Record

### Current defect

ADR-0141 requires AAR-0141 to list exact tuple codes and durable synthesis logs.
The AAR gives only symbolic descriptions for `x`, `y`, `p`, and `q`. At review
time the four specifically advertised files were absent:

```text
test-runs/adr0141-total-multiplication-test.log
test-runs/adr0141-xtab-or-lem-axiom-test.log
test-runs/adr0141-tab-2-or-stronger-test.log
test-runs/adr0141-evidence-ledger-test.log
```

An aggregate slow-evidence log exists and reports passing assertions and
timings, but it does not supply the missing per-variant tuple/proof record.
ADR-0141 also still says `AAR: pending` although AAR-0141 exists.

### Why this is incorrect

Test counts show that the current implementation accepts its fixtures; they do
not make the claimed certificates independently inspectable or replayable.
Missing artifacts and symbolic placeholders prevent an interdeveloper audit of
the exact evidence required by the ADR.

### Correct implementation criteria

- Record exact code terms or canonical values for every `(x,y,p,q)` tuple for
  all three variants, including decoded proof-object structure.
- Preserve one durable synthesis record per variant with the exact command,
  bounds, stdout/stderr, result, elapsed time, and resource usage.
- Ensure every path named by AAR-0141 exists or replace the names with links to
  the actual durable records. A checked-in manifest may index ignored logs but
  cannot replace them.
- Update ADR-0141's AAR field and the completion narrative only after the
  corrected runs finish.

## 6. Unsupported Workstream C Consistency-Preservation Claim

### Current defect

ADR-0119 says the pair/list work provides a consistency-preserving reflected
data-structure extension. The implementation validates formula class,
reflected membership, generated system identity, and changed SelfCons content.
`validate-reflected-basis!` explicitly states that truth of beta in the
standard model remains an external premise and that the builder checks only the
class.

### Why this is incorrect

Changing the reflected beta and regenerated SelfCons text proves that an
extension was encoded. It does not prove that the extension preserves
consistency. Formula-class membership is a syntactic precondition, not a model,
interpretation, conservativity, or relative-consistency argument.

### Correct implementation criteria

- State the exact baseline consistency premise and the preservation theorem
  claimed for the reflected extension.
- Establish that the pair/list beta axioms are true in the selected standard
  model, or provide an interpretation/model-extension argument showing that
  every baseline model extends to satisfy them.
- Connect that semantic result to the exact reflected formulas included in the
  generated system and SelfCons sentence.
- Retain the existing identity and regeneration tests as necessary syntactic
  evidence, but do not treat them as the consistency proof.
- Cite or provide an auditable proof of the preservation step before closing
  Workstream C.

## Why Ordinary SelfCons Axiom Certificates Do Not Qualify

An ordinary `sjas-axiom` citation of the generated Group-3 SelfCons sentence
proves only that the positive SelfCons sentence is an axiom of the generated
system. Every correctly generated baseline system is expected to admit that
citation, so it is not specific evidence of a Goedel-boundary failure.

The negative target has a different obligation: exhibit concrete
`(x,y,p,q)` satisfying the positive body of `not(SelfCons(S))`, where `x` and
`y` are complementary formulas and `p` and `q` are accepted proof objects for
those formulas in the exact measured proof relation of `S`. The proof for the
contradictory member must execute the unsafe variant's reduced-witness route.
A proof of positive SelfCons does none of this and points in the opposite
logical direction.

This does not prohibit every ordinary axiom leaf inside a larger valid
counterexample. An axiom citation may discharge a component that genuinely is
in reflected beta and is allowed by the target's positive body. It cannot, by
itself or by relabeling, serve as the boundary contradiction certificate, the
reduced-witness derivation, or the independent synthesis evidence.

## Required Reopening and Completion Order

1. Reopen ADR-0119 Workstreams B and C and retract the current completion
   assertions.
2. Remove the trusted contradiction constructor and build ordinary checked
   derivations for all three boundary variants.
3. Implement genuinely independent proof-object synthesis and preserve its
   durable outputs.
4. Harden the ledger against report-shaped metadata forgery and unify all
   public completion surfaces.
5. Supply the exact tuple/proof records and the Workstream C preservation
   argument.
6. Rerun focused semantic selectors, the fast and extended gates, focused SJAS
   progression, and the complete long-running evidence probes before issuing a
   corrected AAR and completion claim.

The core acceptance condition is not a passing test count. It is an auditable,
ordinary derivation of each contradiction plus an independently discovered
proof tuple, both checked by the exact object-level apparatus whose boundary is
being demonstrated.
